# GRIT roadmap

Where GRIT is heading, what's done, and the product decisions behind it.
Last updated November 2026.

## Decisions

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Social privacy default | **Friends-only.** New accounts are private: follows need approval and only approved followers see your activity. Blocking is included. |
| Paid tier | **None for now.** The 5-habit "free tier" cap is gone (a sanity limit of 20 remains). Revisit premium once the core features are done. |
| Workout plan | **Generated by the coach** from the onboarding answers, editable afterwards. |
| Light theme | **Dark only for now.** Colours live in `apps/mobile/constants/colors.ts`, so adding light mode later stays cheap. |

## Phase 0 — Bug fixes ✅

Streaks capped at ~37 days, coach history showing the oldest messages, feed
paging skipping items, future-dated logs, the coach's daily limit race, the
splash screen hanging on a font error, the floating tab bar covering bottom
sheets, keyboard handling in sheets, safe-area insets, pull-to-refresh, and
display rounding.

## Phase 1 — Core features ✅

- **Onboarding answers are saved** (goal weight, pace, macro focus, diet,
  aim, obstacle, earnback, rollover, units, notification preferences) and
  used: macro targets follow the focus, the day's calorie budget adds
  burned calories and up to 200 kcal rolled over, and the coach knows them.
- **Settings:** Goals & Targets with a recommended calorie goal,
  Notifications (daily workout and meal reminders on the device), and
  Account (units, password reset, delete account).
- **History and editing:** browse earlier days of food and weeks of stats,
  log against past days, edit or delete food entries and workouts, undo
  habit check-ins and back-fill the last 30 days, rename habits.
- **Workout plan** built by the coach, with a Plan screen, today's session
  on Home, a day editor and rebuilding on request.
- **Loading, error and failure states** on every screen, instant feedback
  for check-ins and deletes, and toasts when an action fails.
- Habit streaks load in one request instead of one per habit.

## Phase 2 — Engagement ✅

- **Friends-only social:** accounts are private by default; follows of a
  private account are requests the owner accepts or declines. Only
  approved followers see activity, weekly stats and follower lists, or
  can give kudos. Search by name or @username, remove followers, block
  (removes follows both ways and hides each from the other), profile
  photos.
- **Notifications:** an inbox for follows, requests, kudos, challenge
  joins and results, streak-at-risk (8pm) and the weekly check-in
  (Sundays 6pm), at each user's local time, with Expo push to every
  signed-in phone. "Only when off track" applies to the weekly check-in;
  friends' activity pushes can be turned off.
- **Weight tracking:** daily weigh-ins, progress to the goal weight.
- **Stats:** 7-day / 30-day / 3-month trends for calories, protein,
  activity and weight.
- **Challenges:** active minutes, workouts or steps, counted from logged
  workouts (walks, runs and hikes take a step count); results and places
  when they end; leave and delete.
- **Recent and favourite foods** for logging again in a tap.

## Phase 3 — Production readiness ✅

- **Backend:** environment checked at boot, `GET /health` (used by
  Railway), sign-in tokens verified locally against Supabase's published
  key, rate limiting (300 requests a minute per session), request IDs
  and one JSON log line per request, an exception filter that hides
  internals, crash reports from the app (`POST /client-errors`), graceful
  shutdown, missing indexes and caps on unbounded lists.
- **Mobile:** a recovery screen instead of a blank app when a screen
  crashes, offline handling (cached screens, a banner, saves fail fast),
  store config fixes (HTTPS-only store builds, no microphone permission,
  an iOS privacy manifest, unused HealthKit packages removed) and
  accessibility roles, labels and states throughout.
- **Tests and CI:** GitHub Actions runs the backend (migrations, drift
  check, format, lint, types, unit and database tests, build) and the
  mobile app (types, lint, unit tests) on every pull request. Database
  tests cover the feed, leaderboard, privacy, alerts, challenges, weight
  and quick foods.

## Phase 4 — Moat features and platform ✅

Chosen from [`MARKET_RESEARCH.md`](./MARKET_RESEARCH.md): the three
"build first" features, plus the platform work that was asked for.

- **Light theme.** Follows the phone's setting, or pick Light or Dark
  under Account → Appearance.
- **Google and Apple sign-in** on the login and sign-up screens (native
  Apple sign-in on iPhone, a browser sign-in everywhere else). The buttons
  only appear for providers that are switched on in Supabase.
- **Apple Health / Health Connect sync.** Workouts, steps, sleep and
  weigh-ins come in automatically (Settings → Apple Health / Health
  Connect). Imports never duplicate, steps count toward step challenges,
  and the coach sees steps and sleep.
- **Adaptive calorie targets.** From logged intake and the weigh-in
  trend, GRIT estimates what you actually burn and suggests a new target
  on Stats and in the Sunday check-in. You can use it, change it or snooze
  it. Changes are capped at ±200 kcal a week, with a 1,200 kcal floor and
  a settling period after each change.
- **✨ Smart log.** Type, dictate or photograph a meal. GRIT splits it
  into items with portions, including hidden oil, butter and sauces.
  Nutrition comes first from your own past confirmations, then the food
  database for branded items, then a checked estimate, and each item shows
  its source. You review every item before logging. Confirmed foods and
  portions are remembered ("Foods GRIT knows" in Profile), so logging gets
  faster with use. Capped at 30 readings a day.
- **Coach memory.** The coach notes lasting facts from chats (injuries,
  foods avoided, when you can train), replaces notes that go out of date,
  and uses them in every reply. It also sees your week's averages and the
  adaptive estimate. Notes appear in the chat as they're taken and can be
  viewed, added and deleted under "What your coach knows".
- **Proactive check-ins.** At 1pm local time the coach checks for a
  logging gap, a run of missed workouts, low protein, or a weight plateau
  while cutting, and sends the first that applies. Each kind goes at most
  once a week, never on Sundays, and only with progress alerts on.

Database: migrations `20261106120000` to `20261106150000` are applied to
Supabase. All are additive, so earlier servers keep working.

## Phase 5 — Own food and exercise data, and ML (planned)

See [`PHASE5_PLAN.md`](./PHASE5_PLAN.md), shaped by the research in
[`reports/GRIT health fitness nutrition moat.md`](../reports/GRIT%20health%20fitness%20nutrition%20moat.md).
It starts with a **trust and safety baseline** (no third-party SDKs on
health screens, AI consent naming Microsoft, the EU AI disclosure, an 18+
gate, calorie floors), then two tracks:

- **Nutrition:** GRIT's own food database built from open sources (USDA,
  Open Food Facts, UK, Canada, France and Finland tables, with regional
  tables added where users' searches miss),
  local search and barcodes, micronutrient tracking, models for matching
  food names, typical portions and estimating missing micronutrients, and
  label-photo contributions with quality checks. Smart log gains confidence
  badges and a cooking-oil question; caffeine and alcohol are tracked;
  nutrition is written back to the phone's health app.
- **Fitness:** an exercise library that coach plans are built from (with
  injury-aware filtering), set-by-set strength logging with PRs, calorie
  burn from intensity and heart rate calibrated against each person's
  weight trend (with a cycle-aware filter), strength levels from
  OpenPowerlifting, voice and offline set logging, a wider health import
  (HRV, resting heart rate, VO2max, cycle data), and progression rules that
  switch to "preserve" in a big deficit or when protein is low.

## Later

- From the research: Squads (small accountability groups), a GLP-1
  mode, and strength tracking with progressive overload linked to
  nutrition.
- Let the coach propose changes to the workout plan and habits for the
  user to approve, as it already does for calorie targets.
- A real-device check of push alerts, photo picking and the keyboard in
  sheets.
- Fuzzy user search (a `pg_trgm` index) once there are many users.
- Commit `package-lock.json` (it's git-ignored today) so CI and Railway
  install the same versions.

## Needs the account owner

- Google and Apple sign-in: enable the Google and Apple providers in
  Supabase Auth. Add `grit://auth-callback` and the web app's
  `/auth-callback` URL to the redirect URLs. Apple needs a Services ID and
  key from the Apple Developer account; Google needs an OAuth client.
  Until then the buttons stay hidden.
- HealthKit / Health Connect and Smart log's camera: these need a
  development build (`eas build --profile development`) on real devices,
  since Expo Go has no HealthKit or Health Connect. Check the permission
  prompts, a first sync, and a photo reading.
- Push notifications: the app already has an EAS project ID; set
  `EXPO_ACCESS_TOKEN` on the server if push security is turned on for
  the Expo project. Crash reports go to the backend logs; add Sentry
  later if you want dashboards and alerting.
- Railway: confirm the health check uses `/health` (set in
  `railway.toml`) and optionally set `CORS_ORIGINS` to the web app's URL.
- Password reset: add `grit://reset-password` (and the web app's
  `/reset-password` URL) to Supabase Auth's redirect URLs.
